About Us

What is SEALED?

SEALED is a barangay document request and management system that connects residents, staff, and administrators in one secure platform — making public service faster, transparent, and accessible from anywhere.

Why SEALED Exists

Built around three commitments to the community it serves.

Secure by Design

Every resident account is verified by barangay staff before access is granted, and every issued document carries a verification code anyone can validate.

Faster Service

Request and track documents online instead of falling in line. Real-time status updates mean you know exactly when your document is ready for pickup.

Serving Every Barangay

Administrators manage multiple barangays on one platform, each with its own records, document types, staff, and identity.

How It Works

Four simple steps from registration to document collection.

1

Register

Create your account with your personal information and select your barangay.

2

Get Verified

Barangay staff reviews and approves your account. You'll be notified once verified.

3

Request Document

Choose the document type, state your purpose, and submit your request online.

4

Collect

Track your request status and pick up your document when it's ready.
